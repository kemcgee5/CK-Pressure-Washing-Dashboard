Payments & Policies

Payment

Payment is due after the work is completed, per your invoice. We accept credit and debit cards through Stripe’s secure checkout, as well as check, cash and Zelle. Card payments may include a small processing fee, which is shown on your invoice before you pay. We do not store your card details.

Cancellations & Rescheduling

Please give us at least 24 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule an appointment. Weather may require us to reschedule, and we will contact you as soon as possible.

Satisfaction & Refunds

If you are not satisfied with a completed job, contact us within 7 days and we will return to correct it at no charge. If we cannot resolve it, we will work with you on a fair adjustment or refund of the affected portion. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method.

Privacy

We use your name, address, phone and email only to quote, schedule, perform and bill your job. We do not sell or share your information, except with payment processors such as Stripe to complete a payment.
